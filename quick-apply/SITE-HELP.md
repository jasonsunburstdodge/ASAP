# ASAP site help

Reviewed November 10, 2026. The public WordPress page/post navigation index excludes the obsolete home and thank-you page. Job listings remain in the separate job snapshot.

`site-help.js` contains curated English/Spanish answers with official source destinations. `site-index.js` supports title-based navigation, not semantic answers from article bodies. Unknown or ambiguous questions receive a recruiter/contact handoff. No external AI service receives applicant information through this module.

Use Ask about ASAP from any stage; Return to my application preserves the stage and answers. Clearly identified company questions can also open help from the normal answer field. Voice input/output use the existing consent and language controls.

Run `python quick-apply/scripts/refresh-site-index.py` from the repository root to refresh navigation. Review source pages and manually update curated answers when facts change. This is not an automatic live crawl or unrestricted AI Q&A. Do not add unverified guarantees, eligibility decisions or personal payroll answers.

Sources: ASAP official About Us, Contact Us, Job Seeker FAQ, For Employers, General Application, Job Openings, Privacy Policy and job seeker Resource Library pages. Austin/Dallas temporary-domain email addresses were deliberately omitted pending confirmation.
